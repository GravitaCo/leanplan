# Marketing to people stepping down from weight-loss medicine: proposal

`marketing-research`, 11 Oct 2026. Proposal only. Labels: **[E]** evidence, **[I]** inference, **[U]** unknown. UK unless marked. Works within the limits in `glp1-compliance.md`: in marketing, no medicine names, no "GLP-1", jab, injection or pen, and no paid targeting by interest in the medicine.

**The answer:** we market a moment, not a medicine. "Keep what's working, calmly" reaches this group through communities, independent professionals and useful free content. Paid ads aimed at them are out. Confidence: medium on the need, low on reach and willingness to pay until the interviews and page test are done.

## 1. The audience

- **How many use these medicines.** About 1.5m UK users in March 2025 (IQVIA, via Pulse). 1.6m GB adults in the year to early 2025 (UCL, *BMC Medicine*, Jan 2026). A press estimate of almost 2.5m buyers in July has an unverified year [E, mixed]. About 80 to 90% buy privately (IQVIA; thinkmoney/Savanta, Jan 2026) [E].
- **Who.** About twice as many women as men, peaking at ages 45 to 55, and more common among people reporting distress (UCL) [E]. Take-up skews middle class (Irish News, citing a 2026 survey) [E, press].
- **How many stop.** UK: **unknown**. In the US, 65% of people without diabetes stopped within a year (Rodriguez, *JAMA Netw Open* 2025) [E, US]. Cost pushes people to stop: users spend £210 a month on average, 39% use credit, and 21% say a further price rise would make them stop (thinkmoney/Savanta, Jan 2026, commissioned) [E, weak]. **[I]** That suggests several hundred thousand people step down each year. This is the number to firm up.
- **Where they look for help.**
  - Their provider's app and coach, which usually ends when the prescription does [I].
  - Reddit: r/mounjarouk has about 59k members and r/mounjaromaintenanceuk about 9k, with "maintenance" the top topic (GummySearch, 30 Sep 2026) [E].
  - Facebook groups and TikTok: sizes **unknown**.
  - Google, community pharmacists and GPs. NICE expects a year of follow-up, but only for NHS patients (NICE, Aug 2025) [E].

## 2. Positioning and the core message

**Positioning:** Tali is the calm, independent place to keep your habits steady after a big change. Sleep, food and movement are in one place, with ranges, not rules. Tali works whatever helped you get here, and it stays with you after any programme ends.

**Core message:** you've made a big change, and now you want to keep it without going back to weighing every meal.

**Candidate taglines.** All need `compliance` sign-off and free CAP Copy Advice. None names a medicine or promises a weight result.
1. "Keep what's working."
2. "Steady is the goal."
3. "Ranges, not rules. For whatever comes next."
4. "Hungry days happen. Have a plan for them."
5. "When the extra help stops, keep the habits that help." *(This is closest to the line. Ask CAP about "extra help" first.)*

**Supporting lines:** "Short strength sessions at home." "A simple protein range that fits your week." "No streaks. No red numbers. Miss a week, and it's welcome back."

**Never say:** maintain or protect your weight loss, prevent regain, rebound, taper, off the jab, quit, cheating, "don't undo it", or "programme" (the compliance and mind lists). **Ask `compliance` about "food noise".** It is the community's own phrase, but it is so closely tied to these medicines that it may count as a reference to them.

## 3. Channels

**Allowed, ranked by value for cost:**

1. **Community, as a member rather than an advertiser.** Answer maintenance questions with general habit help. Disclose that you are from Tali. Post only where the moderators allow it. The same advertising rules apply to Tali's own posts and replies. Cost: time.
2. **Independent professionals:** dietitians, nutritionists and PTs who don't prescribe or sell the medicine. They can give a client Tali one-to-one; that is a referral, not an endorsement. No endorsements in ads (CAP 12.18). Pay no commission at first. No data passes between Tali and the partner. Cost: time.
3. **A free in-browser tool**, "Build your hungry-days plan". It gives if-then plans and a protein range, with no sign-up and nothing stored. It is useful enough to share and earns links. `nutrition-accuracy` checks the ranges. Cost: a few days of build.
4. **SEO on habit topics, with no medicine names:**
   - protein at every meal, made simple
   - strength training at home for beginners
   - what to do on hungry days
   - sleep, stress and appetite
   - keeping habits steady without counting calories
   - what a weekly review is

   **[I]** Tali can't use medicine-name searches such as "coming off ...". Accept that, and don't use workarounds.
5. **App store, when a store build exists.** Health & Fitness category. Keywords: habits, protein, strength, steady, maintenance. No trademarks in metadata (Apple 2.3.7, Google Play metadata policy).
6. **Partner sharing.** The user chooses to share their week plan with a partner or friend. Tali sends no invites (PECR) and gives no rewards.

**Grey: prescribers and pharmacies.** A provider that sells the medicine and also promotes Tali risks making a combined advert for a prescription-only medicine. Juniper's ad was upheld even though it covered coaching and an app. A clinician handing Tali to a patient during care is probably fine. Any co-branded material, or listing on a provider's website, goes to `compliance` first. NHS services have to go through procurement, so they come later.

**Off limits:**
- paid social or search targeted at interest in the medicine
- medicine-name keywords and hashtags
- pen or needle images, and before and after photos
- influencer deals tied to the medicines (ASA, Jul 2025)
- weight-result promises
- health professional endorsements in ads
- anything aimed at under-18s
- using health data for ad targeting
- streaks, countdowns and fake urgency

## 4. What the app must do: top 5 jobs, ranked

1. **"Help me handle my appetite coming back without panicking."**
   - Have: hungry-days if-then plans, and sleep and stress pattern lines.
   - Gap: the optional "food thoughts" check-in item, to pilot. Copy promises no timeline.
2. **"Give me a way of eating that isn't counting."**
   - Have: the protein range (1.2 to 1.6 g/kg for Maintain), usual meals and ranges with a ± margin.
   - Gap: faster logging (say or type the meal).
   - Fix the protein split so it does not reveal medication status.
3. **"Help me get my strength back."** The "Strong and steady" 12-week plan (`glp1-fitness.md`). Its name is neutral.
4. **"Let me spot drift early, without the scale running my life."**
   - Have: the weight opt-in, the steady range and the 4-week trend in words, all approved.
   - Keep "Make this my new starting point".
   - Be honest: Tali supports habits and can't promise a result.
5. **"Don't make me feel like a failure when life gets in the way."**
   - Have: "welcome back", counts with no "x of y", no streaks, and gentle mode.
   - Gap: the Keep it steady guide with its prescriber and Beat lines, clinician-reviewed before launch.

**Underneath all five:** Tali never asks about or stores medication status. People find the right path through the Keep it steady goal.

## 5. What to measure

Tali does not store medication, so the **Keep it steady goal cohort is the stand-in**. It also includes people who never took a medicine. Report results by cohort and in aggregate only. Any new analytics is a `compliance` question.

- **Activation in week 1:** 3 or more check-ins, one session, and one if-then plan chosen.
- **Retention:** weekly reviews opened at weeks 4, 8 and 12. Day-30 and day-90 active.
- **Habits:**
  - strength sessions per week (share with 2 or more)
  - protein days in range (count, not "x of y")
  - if-then plans used in reviews
- **Drift response:** the share who choose "new starting point" against the share who drop out after a drift.
- **Safety:** support sheet opens and wellbeing-flag rate. These are guardrails, not targets.
- **Growth:**
  - cost per user retained at day 30, by channel
  - the plan-sharing rate
  - waitlist-to-interview rate on the page test
- **Not measured as success:** downloads, sign-ups or kg.

## Top 3 decisions for Benn

1. **Market the moment, never the medicine.** Approve "Keep what's working" (or pick another) as the lead line, subject to CAP Copy Advice, and confirm there will be no paid targeting of this group.
2. **Community and independent professionals first.** Approve 15 interviews and one dietitian or PT partner pilot (research data goes past `compliance` first). Approve the free hungry-days tool. Hold any prescriber or pharmacy tie-up until `compliance` clears it.
3. **Measure the Keep it steady cohort on retention and habits, not weight.** Make the week-12 retention and strength-habit numbers the go or no-go for widening spend.
