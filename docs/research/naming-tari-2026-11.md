# Tari: deep dive (November 2026)

Author: `marketing-research` agent, for Benn. Checked 2 November 2026. Not committed. **Not legal advice.**
Labels: **[E]** the evidence shows, **[I]** my inference, **[G]** a guess that needs testing.
Brief: Benn picked Tari because it is one letter from Tali, so the brand, mark and feel can carry over. He is not worried about merely similar names. What matters is identical or near-identical TARI marks in our field, real-world brands that would dominate the name, and the US eventually.

---

## Verdict

**Proceed with caution, and take it to an attorney. On balance Tilo is the stronger choice.**

**What looks good:** the registers are friendlier than I expected.
- The USPTO has **no live TARI mark in classes 9, 10, 41, 42 or 44**.
- The live US TARI marks cover cryptocurrency (class 36), sauces, T-shirts and food chemicals.

**Three things stand against it.** None of them shows up on a register search alone.

**The three biggest risks**

1. **Tari already belongs to a crypto project, online and in the app stores.**
   - Tari Labs owns **tari.com**. It runs the Tari privacy blockchain (mainnet launched 6 May 2025) and the **"Tari Universe Wallet"** iOS app, updated 23 Oct 2026.
   - Its company, Tari, LLC, holds a live US TARI registration for crypto tokens (class 36).
   - [I] An app called Tari will sit under a crypto brand in search and on social media. A crypto connotation is a poor match for "clear and trustworthy".
2. **It sits close to TALI, so it may not solve the original problem.**
   - TALI Health Pty Ltd holds live US registrations for TALI in classes 9, 10, 44 (reg. 87982698) and 41, 42 (reg. 87135228).
   - TARI differs by one consonant, and *l* and *r* are close sounds.
   - [I] An examiner or opponent could argue the two are similar. The three existing health Talis also make Tari read as a variant of them rather than a new name.
   - Only an attorney can say whether one letter is enough distance.
3. **Bad meanings in markets that matter to a UK app.**
   - **Italy:** TARI is the national household waste tax (*Tassa sui Rifiuti*), a bill most Italian households pay every year.
   - **Tagalog and Cebuano:** *tari* is the blade tied to a fighting cock's leg. In Cebuano it is also slang for penis and for sex (Wiktionary). [I] Filipino speakers are a sizeable UK community, including in the NHS workforce.
   - **Hindi/Urdu:** *tāṛī* is toddy, a palm alcohol. [I, from knowledge, not checked]

**Also worth knowing:** a US app called **"Tari App"** existed in our exact field. It was a nutrition, fitness and coaching tracker (Greenville, SC; launched on Product Hunt 21 Apr 2020; founder David Andreoli).
- It looks dormant: there is one Trustpilot review, from May 2020, and it did not appear in my US App Store searches on 2 Nov 2026.
- tariapp.com is still registered to someone.
- It has no USPTO filing.
- [I] It is low risk, but it is the closest same-field use I found, so it is a passing-off point for the attorney to check.

## 6. UK-ear check (candid)

[I] Everything in this section is my judgement. The waitlist test should confirm it.

- **Pronunciation splits three ways:**
  - **TAH-ree** (like *safari* or *Atari*): probably what Benn intends and the most common reading.
  - **TARR-ee** (like *tarry*, *carry*, *Harry*).
  - **TAIR-ee** (like *dairy*, *fairy*), which some will take from "tare".
- **Spelling after one hearing:**
  - TAH-ree gives Tari, Tahri, Tarri or Tarry.
  - TARR-ee gives Tarry or Tarrie.
  - That is better than Tilo (fewer competing spellings) but not as clean as Tali. "Tarry" is a real English word, so autocorrect will push people towards it.
- **What people will hear in it:**
  - **Atari** (the games brand): very likely for anyone over 35. Playful, not calm.
  - **tarry** (to linger, or tar-covered): mildly negative, and the opposite of steady progress.
  - **tiara** and **safari**: harmless.
  - **Tory**: a real risk in TARR-ee or TOR-ee accents (some northern and Scottish speakers). It is a party-political echo for a UK consumer brand [G].
  - **sorry** and **starry**: rhymes. Neutral.
  - **tariff**: topical, transactional.
- **How close to Tali:**
  - Written down, Tari and Tali are one letter apart and look almost identical in a logo.
  - [I] To a user who knows Tali Health or Tali AI, Tari will look like a variant rather than a fresh name. That is acceptable if nobody knows them, as in the UK consumer market today. It is less so in US health software, where the Talis operate.
  - [I] On the TALI Health Pty marks, see risk 2: it is a question for the attorney.
- **The hidden word:** Tari loses the thing Benn valued most. "Tally" is gone, and no everyday word tied to the product replaces it. The best hidden meaning, "dance" (Indonesian and Malay), fits only the movement pillar.

## 7. Fit and compliance

- **Friend on my side:** good. Two soft syllables, ends in a vowel, sounds like a name (it is a given name and surname in several cultures).
- **Calm and steady:** weak to neutral. "Atari", "tarry" and the crypto association pull away from calm. Arabic and Hebrew "fresh" and Maltese "tender" are lovely, but nobody in the UK will hear them.
- **Clear and trustworthy:** at risk from the crypto ownership of tari.com and from Tari Psychics on tari.app [I].
- **Compliance:**
  - The name itself makes no health, medical or weight-loss claim.
  - tari.health would lean towards health-as-treatment wording, so prefer tari.fit.
  - [I] Avoid any campaign that plays on "dance" in a way that implies exercise outcomes. That is ordinary ASA/CAP hygiene, not a naming issue.

## 8. Tari vs Tilo

Benn reports that Tilo is clear on the UK, US (exact) and EU registers and that he owns tilo.fit.

| | **Tari** | **Tilo** |
|---|---|---|
| Carry-over from Tali | **Strong.** The logo, sound and rhythm carry over almost unchanged. | Moderate. Same soft two-syllable shape, new letters. |
| Identical marks in our classes (US) | None found | None found (Benn reports UK and EU clear as well) |
| Near-identical wellbeing marks (US) | None in our classes | **TILA** (class 9, a meditation and sleep app) and **TEELO** (classes 9 and 42; opposition window 27 Oct 2026) |
| Distance from the TALI marks | **Close** (one letter) | Clear |
| Who dominates the name online | **Tari Labs crypto** (tari.com, an active iOS wallet); the Italian waste tax; Indonesian dance | An Austrian flooring company (tilo.com); several small apps |
| Same-field users | A dormant 2020 US nutrition and coaching app called Tari App | None in nutrition. A calm-tone time logger and a dormant B2B stress app (GetTilo) |
| Bad meanings | **Tagalog and Cebuano cockfighting blade, plus Cebuano sexual slang**; Italian bin tax; Hindi toddy; French "dried up" | None found |
| UK ear | Atari, tarry, Tory (accent-dependent) | Kilo, and three-way pronunciation |
| Hidden meaning | Dance (Indonesian); fresh (Arabic, Hebrew) | Linden and calming tea; court linden (trust) |
| .fit domain | tari.fit probably available | tilo.fit owned |

**My read [I]:** **Tilo is the stronger name.**
- Tari's one real advantage is carry-over: no rebrand of the mark or palette.
- Against that, Tari brings a dominant crypto owner, closeness to the TALI marks that started this exercise, and negative meanings in two languages spoken by large UK communities.
- Tilo's weaknesses (TILA and TEELO in the US, the "kilo" echo, mixed pronunciation) are real, but they are narrower. Benn has already cleared Tilo on the registers he can see.
- If carry-over matters most, Tari is worth one attorney question: "Is TARI far enough from the TALI registrations, and will Tari, LLC oppose a class 9 filing?" If either answer is shaky, stop.
