# Warm-up clip prompts (Seedance)

For the 19 moves in `WARMUP_CUES` (`src/core/data/warmups.ts`), in that order. Paste each prompt after the character and scene block. Each clip loops, lasts about 8 to 12 seconds, starts and ends in the same pose, and follows the warm-up cue rather than the library cue: easy, flowing, within a comfortable range, lighter effort, nothing ballistic, no jumping, no long holds.

The house rep ending is used with "slow" changed to "easy", because warm-up pace is smooth and continuous rather than the 3-second lowering tempo: "Two easy, controlled repetitions, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. She starts and ends in the same position." Where a move swings or circles by design, the ending says "no jerking or forcing" instead. The march uses the house steady ending. Timings are coaching judgement calls, not measurements; every clip is re-timed from the footage.

### March on the spot with arm swings (`march-on-the-spot`)
- Clip file: `warmup-march-on-the-spot.mp4` · Type: warm-up
- Equipment in scene: none
- Prompt (paste after the character and scene block):
  > Front three-quarter medium-wide shot. She stands tall and marches on the spot at an easy pace, lifting her knees a comfortable height and swinging her arms loosely and naturally, shoulders relaxed. Her breathing picks up a little but she stays relaxed and comfortable, not pushing. She moves at a steady, easy pace the whole time, relaxed and breathing comfortably, as if she could hold a conversation. The clip loops, so her pace and position look the same at the start and end. About 8 to 10 seconds, roughly one step every half second to second, starting and ending with her feet together and arms by her sides.
- Form check: marching fast, jogging or bouncing; stiff arms or shrugged shoulders; drifting across the frame so the loop jumps.
- Notes: a pulse raiser, so it uses the steady ending rather than a rep count. "Starting and ending with her feet together" is added so the loop closes; a mid-stride match would also work. If Seedance reads "hold a conversation" as talking, drop that phrase.

### Step jacks (`step-jacks`)
- Clip file: `warmup-step-jacks.mp4` · Type: warm-up
- Equipment in scene: none
- Prompt (paste after the character and scene block):
  > Front medium-wide shot. She stands tall with her feet together and arms by her sides. She steps her left foot out to the side as her arms rise out to the sides to a comfortable height, about shoulder height or a little above, then steps back in as her arms come down. She then does the same with her right foot. Her steps are light, both feet stay close to the floor and she never jumps. A steady, even rhythm. Two easy, controlled repetitions each side, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. She starts and ends in the same position. Starting standing tall, left, right, left, right in about 8 seconds, a steady rhythm of about 2 seconds per step: about 1 second stepping out as her arms rise and about 1 second stepping back in as they lower.
- Form check: she hops or jumps; her arms are flung up or forced overhead; the rhythm is uneven or her feet stamp.
- Notes: the brief says one each side for alternating moves, but one each side at a warm-up rhythm is only about 4 seconds, so this shows two each side to reach the clip length. Cut to one each side if a shorter loop is fine. The cue says "only as high as is comfortable" without a height; "about shoulder height or a little above" is my judgement, chosen so the clip doesn't model a full overhead reach.

### Arm circles (`arm-circles`)
- Clip file: `warmup-arm-circles.mp4` · Type: warm-up
- Equipment in scene: none
- Prompt (paste after the character and scene block):
  > Front medium shot, framed from the hips up. She stands tall, feet hip-width apart, arms straight out to the sides at shoulder height. She draws small circles forwards with her arms, the second a little bigger than the first, then draws two circles backwards, small then a little bigger. Her shoulders stay low and relaxed, away from her ears, and the circles stay within a comfortable range. Smooth and continuous, no flinging. Two easy circles each way, perfect form, no jerking or forcing. She starts and ends in the same position. Starting with her arms out to the sides, 2 circles forwards and 2 backwards in about 9 seconds, roughly 2 seconds per circle with a brief settle as she changes direction.
- Form check: her shoulders hunch up towards her ears; the circles become big, fast windmills; her arms drop well below shoulder height.
- Notes: the cue says "let them grow" but doesn't say whether the backward circles also grow; I have them start small again. With two circles each way the growth will be subtle. Where her arms go at the start and end (out to the sides) follows the cue's set-up.

### Leg swings (`leg-swings`)
- Clip file: `warmup-leg-swings.mp4` · Type: warm-up
- Equipment in scene: the studio wall
- Prompt (paste after the character and scene block):
  > Side-on medium-wide shot, the wall behind her. She stands tall side-on to the wall with her right hand resting on it. She swings her left leg forwards and back in an easy, loose arc, each swing a little bigger than the one before but never kicking for height. Her body stays tall and her lower back doesn't twist or arch to swing further. After the last swing she brings her left foot down beside her right. Easy, relaxed swings, perfect form, no jerking or forcing. She starts and ends in the same position. Starting standing tall with both feet down, 4 easy swings, each forwards and back, in about 8 seconds, in a smooth, natural pendulum rhythm of about 2 seconds a swing, then she settles with both feet down.
- Form check: she kicks the leg up or forces the height; her lower back arches or twists; she leans heavily on the wall.
- Notes: left leg swinging, shown one side only (mirror for the right side). Two swings take only about 4 seconds, so this shows four to reach the clip length and let "a little bigger each time" show. The house ending's "no bouncing or swinging" contradicts this move, so it says "no jerking or forcing", as in the library prompt.

### World's greatest stretch (`worlds-greatest-stretch`)
- Clip file: `warmup-worlds-greatest-stretch.mp4` · Type: warm-up
- Equipment in scene: a thin grey mat
- Prompt (paste after the character and scene block):
  > Front three-quarter medium-wide shot at low height. She is in a long lunge on a thin grey mat, left foot forward, right knee resting down on the mat, both hands flat on the floor inside her left foot. She reaches her left arm up towards the ceiling, turning her chest to open towards the left and following her hand with her eyes, only as far as is comfortable, then brings the hand back down beside the other. The movement is smooth and continuous, and the turn comes from her upper back; her hips stay low and steady. Two easy, controlled repetitions, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. She starts and ends in the same position. Starting in the lunge with both hands down, 2 easy repetitions in about 10 seconds. Each rep is about a 2-second reach up, a brief moment with the arm raised, about a 2-second return, and a brief settle with both hands down.
- Form check: her hips twist or rise as she turns; her front knee drifts inwards; she swings the arm up fast or forces the turn.
- Notes: left foot forward, left (inside) arm reaching, shown one side only (mirror for the right side). The clip starts already in the lunge; stepping into it isn't shown so the loop stays clean. The cue allows the back knee down or lifted; knee down is shown as the easier option. "Hips stay low and steady" is reused from the library prompt; the warm-up cue doesn't mention the hips.

### Bodyweight squat (`bodyweight-squat`)
- Clip file: `warmup-bodyweight-squat.mp4` · Type: warm-up
- Equipment in scene: none
- Prompt (paste after the character and scene block):
  > Front three-quarter medium-wide shot. She stands tall with her feet about shoulder-width apart, arms reaching forward at shoulder height for balance. She sits her hips back and down into an easy, shallow squat, heels flat, then stands tall. On the second rep she goes a little deeper, still within a comfortable range with her heels down, then stands tall again. Her knees follow her toes and don't cave inwards. Smooth and continuous. Two easy, controlled repetitions, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. She starts and ends in the same position. Starting standing tall, 2 easy repetitions in about 9 seconds. - Notes: the arms reaching forward come from the library prompt; the warm-up cue doesn't say where the arms go. The library's slightly turned-out toes are left out because the warm-up cue doesn't mention them. "Start shallow and go a little deeper each time" is shown across two reps, so the difference in depth should be visible but modest.

### Glute bridge (`glute-bridge`)
- Clip file: `warmup-glute-bridge.mp4` · Type: warm-up
- Equipment in scene: a thin grey mat
- Prompt (paste after the character and scene block):

- Notes: the cue doesn't give a lift height, so the prompt says "a comfortable height" rather than the library's hips-in-line-with-knees-and-shoulders. Feet hip-width and arms by her sides are reused from the library prompt; the warm-up cue doesn't state them.
