## Step 2: exercise prompts

The first 28 are the moves in Tali's three plans: make these first.

### 1. Bird-dog (`bird-dog`)

- Clip file: `bird-dog.mp4` · Type: rep
- Equipment in scene: thin grey mat

```text
Side-on low shot at floor level. She kneels on all fours on a thin grey mat, hands under her shoulders and knees under her hips, back flat and neck neutral, looking at the floor. She slowly reaches her right arm forward and her left leg back until both are level with her body, keeping her back flat and her hips level, pauses, then returns to all fours. She repeats with her left arm and right leg. Her leg never lifts higher than her body, so her lower back does not arch. Two slow, controlled repetitions, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. She starts and ends in the same position. 2 slow repetitions, one each side, in about 13 seconds. Each rep is a 3-second reach of the opposite arm and leg, a 1-second pause at full reach, a 1.5-second return, and a 1-second pause on all fours.
```

- Form check: lower back arching or leg lifted above hip height; hips tipping or rotating to one side; same arm and leg reaching instead of opposite ones.
- Notes: alternates sides within the set like the house dead bug, so both sides are shown rather than mirrored.

### 2. Glute bridge (`glute-bridge`)

- Clip file: `glute-bridge.mp4` · Type: rep
- Equipment in scene: thin grey mat

```text
Side-on low shot at floor level. She lies on her back on a thin grey mat, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart close to her bottom, arms resting on the mat by her sides. She presses through her heels and lifts her hips until they are in line with her knees and shoulders, squeezes her glutes at the top, then lowers her hips slowly back to the mat. Her weight stays on her upper back, not her neck, and her lower back does not over-arch at the top. Two slow, controlled repetitions, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. She starts and ends in the same position. 2 slow repetitions in about 13 seconds. Each rep is a 1.5-second lift, a 1-second glute squeeze at the top, a 3-second lowering, and a 1-second pause with her hips resting on the mat.
```

- Form check: hips pushed past the shoulder-knee line so the lower back arches; weight rolling onto the neck or head; heels lifting.

### 3. Incline push-up (`incline-push-up`)

- Clip file: `incline-push-up.mp4` · Type: rep
- Equipment in scene: one sturdy flat bench

```text
Side-on wide shot. The whole body is in frame from head to feet, because the legs and hips matter in this move. The equipment she uses is a sturdy flat bench. Her hands rest on the edge of the bench a little wider than her shoulders, arms straight, feet together on the floor behind her, legs straight and knees off the floor, body in a straight line from head to heels. It is clear she is not kneeling. She lowers her chest towards the edge of the bench with her elbows angled back, not flared out, then pushes herself away until her arms are straight again. Her hips stay in line with her shoulders throughout, neither sagging nor piked up. Two slow, controlled repetitions, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. She starts and ends in the same position. Starting with her arms straight, 2 slow repetitions in about 13 seconds. Each rep is a 3-second lowering to the bench edge, a 1-second pause, a 1.5-second push away, and a 1-second pause with arms straight.
```

- Form check: her feet or knees out of frame; hips sagging or piking up; elbows flaring straight out to the sides; the bench sliding or looking unstable.
- Notes: the cue also allows a worktop or step, and holding dumbbell handles for straighter wrists. The clip shows the plain bench version with hands flat, to keep it simple.

### 4. Side plank (knees) (`side-plank-knees`)

- Clip file: `side-plank-knees.mp4` · Type: hold (one side)
- Equipment in scene: thin grey mat

