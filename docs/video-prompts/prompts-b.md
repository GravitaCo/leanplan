# Seedance prompts: exercise library, slice B

Prompts for 62 exercises from `slice-b.json`, written to the house rules in
`docs/exercise-video-prompts.md`. Nothing here has been generated or checked against footage yet.

## How to use each prompt

1. Paste the **character and scene block** from `docs/exercise-video-prompts.md` exactly as it is.
2. Paste the entry's prompt after it. The prompt already contains the machine or cable add-on (where
   the move needs one), the ending line and the timing text.
3. Generate in vertical 9:16 with a static camera, then re-time and form-check the clip as the tips
   in that doc describe.

## Ending lines used

- **Rep clips** end with the doc's line, word for word: "Two slow, controlled repetitions, perfect
  form, no bouncing or swinging. She starts and ends in the same position." Then the timing text.
  Rep timings use the doc's tempo (3-second lowering, 1-second pause in the stretch, 1.5-second
  lift, 1-second hold, about 13 seconds for two reps) unless an entry says otherwise and why.
- **Hold clips** have no line in the doc for this format, so they all end with the same new line:
  "One steady hold, perfect form, no shaking or shifting. She starts and ends in the same
  position." The timing text says she gets into position over about 2 seconds, holds for about 8
  to 10 seconds and returns over about 2 seconds.
- **Steady clips** use the doc's cardio line, word for word: "She moves at a steady, easy pace the
  whole time, relaxed and breathing comfortably, as if she could hold a conversation. The clip
  loops, so her pace and position look the same at the start and end." Timing text: about 8 to 10
  seconds.

## Things to decide before generating

- **Hold length.** The doc's plank prompt asks for a 20-second hold with no getting in or out. This
  brief asks for 8 to 10 seconds with getting into position and returning, so the plank entry
  below follows the brief. If this is adopted, the plank section in the doc needs updating to match.
- **Cardio clip length.** The doc aims for 10 to 12 seconds; this brief asks for 8 to 10, and the
  entries below use 8 to 10. The rower's stroke count is cut from 4 to 3 to fit.
- **File names.** Clip files here are `<id>.mp4`. Five of the doc's cardio entries use different
  names for what look like the same exercises: `brisk-walk.mp4` (here `cardio-walk.mp4`),
  `incline-treadmill.mp4` (`cardio-incline-walk.mp4`), `stationary-bike.mp4` (`cardio-bike.mp4`),
  `cross-trainer.mp4` (`cardio-cross-trainer.mp4`) and `rower.mp4` (`cardio-row.mp4`). Pick one
  name for each before a clip is made.
- **Alternating moves.** Following the doc's dead bug ("2 slow repetitions, one each side"), a move
  that alternates sides shows one rep on each side. Whether the app counts a left and a right as
  one rep or two isn't in the data. It affects the counter on cossack squats, mountain climbers,
  step jacks, hip 90/90 and knee rolls.
- **Props added to the scene.** Some moves need a doorway, a post, a table, a box or towels. Each
  one is named in its entry. They slightly change the "plain minimalist studio", so check that
  they look consistent across clips.
- **No clip written:** Swim (see its entry).

- Equipment in scene: adjustable bench set to an incline, two dumbbells
- Prompt (paste after the character and scene block):
  > Side-on wide shot. The whole body is in frame from head to feet, because the legs and hips matter in this move. She rows the dumbbells up towards her hips, squeezing her shoulder blades, then lowers them slowly until her arms hang straight. Her chest stays on the pad; no jerking.
