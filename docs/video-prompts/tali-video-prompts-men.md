## Step 2: exercise prompts

The first 28 are the moves in Tali's three plans: make these first.

### 1. Bird-dog (`bird-dog`)

- Clip file: `bird-dog.mp4` · Type: rep
- Equipment in scene: thin grey mat

```text
Side-on low shot at floor level. He kneels on all fours on a thin grey mat, hands under his shoulders and knees under his hips, back flat and neck neutral, looking at the floor. He slowly reaches his right arm forward and his left leg back until both are level with his body, keeping his back flat and his hips level, pauses, then returns to all fours. He repeats with his left arm and right leg. His leg never lifts higher than his body, so his lower back does not arch. Two slow, controlled repetitions, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. He starts and ends in the same position. 2 slow repetitions, one each side, in about 13 seconds. Each rep is a 3-second reach of the opposite arm and leg, a 1-second pause at full reach, a 1.5-second return, and a 1-second pause on all fours.
```

- Form check: lower back arching or leg lifted above hip height; hips tipping or rotating to one side; same arm and leg reaching instead of opposite ones.
- Notes: alternates sides within the set like the house dead bug, so both sides are shown rather than mirrored.

### 2. Glute bridge (`glute-bridge`)

- Clip file: `glute-bridge.mp4` · Type: rep
- Equipment in scene: thin grey mat

```text
Side-on low shot at floor level. He lies on his back on a thin grey mat, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart close to his bottom, arms resting on the mat by his sides. He presses through his heels and lifts his hips until they are in line with his knees and shoulders, squeezes his glutes at the top, then lowers his hips slowly back to the mat. His weight stays on his upper back, not his neck, and his lower back does not over-arch at the top. Two slow, controlled repetitions, perfect form, no bouncing or swinging. He starts and ends in the same position. 2 slow repetitions in about 13 seconds. Each rep is a 1.5-second lift, a 1-second glute squeeze at the top, a 3-second lowering, and a 1-second pause with his hips resting on the mat.
```

- Form check: hips pushed past the shoulder-knee line so the lower back arches; weight rolling onto the neck or head; heels lifting.

### 3. Incline push-up (`incline-push-up`)

- Clip file: `incline-push-up.mp4` · Type: rep
- Equipment in scene: one sturdy flat bench

```text
Side-on wide shot. The whole body is in frame from head to feet, because the legs and hips matter in this move. The equipment he uses is a sturdy flat bench. His hands rest on the edge of the bench a little wider than his shoulders, arms straight, feet together on the floor behind him, legs straight and knees off the floor, body in a straight line from head to heels. ```
```

- Form check: his feet or knees out of frame; hips sagging or piking up; elbows flaring straight out to the sides; the bench sliding or looking unstable.
- Notes: the cue also allows a worktop or step, and holding dumbbell handles for straighter wrists. The clip shows the plain bench version with hands flat, to keep it simple.
